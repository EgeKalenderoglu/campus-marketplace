Mission Statement

College students often need an affordable and convenient way to buy and sell textbooks, electronics, furniture, clothes, and other goods. There are existing online marketplaces, but they are designed for the public instead of specifically for college students. This can force students to meet up with strangers off campus, and they must determine on their own if the listing can be trusted or not. Campus Marketplace provides a safe marketplace environment for college students. Users are verified using their student email to create a more trusted community. The application will also include designated campus meetup locations, in-app communication, and AI moderation to keep transactions safe and convenient. The platform also has the potential to be monetized by allowing users to promote listings, run ads, or premium features. Campus Marketplace can give students an alternative to larger marketplaces while addressing the safety and convenience issues associated with buying and selling locally.
